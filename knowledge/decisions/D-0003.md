# D-0003 — The sidebar stacks on short screens

Accepted 2026-09-22.

Rusty sent a screenshot of the atlas on their ultrawide (3284x1292, GUI scale 5, 258 GUI
rows) with the location list drawn over "Choose a destination". The sidebar's fixed
layout needs 301 rows: controls to row 128, one list row, a gap, and a 142-row detail block
anchored to the bottom. Vanilla guarantees only 240 rows, and 1080p at the auto scale of 4
is 270, so most players would meet this.

Two fixes were weighed. Compressing the detail block and shrinking the list cannot reach
240 rows: with a location selected the block alone needs more than the sidebar has. A
second sidebar column needs width that 1080p does not have. The accepted design keeps the
tall layout unchanged (list above bottom-anchored details) and, below 301 rows, shows the
list and the details in turn: the list fills the sidebar while nothing is selected, and a
selection replaces it with the details under a "Back to list" button, which deselects. The
details block is top-anchored at row 107 with the buttons pulled up, fitting 240 rows.

Layout is recomputed every frame from the height and the selection, so widgets move and
hide without rebuilding the screen. The booth switches to GUI scale 3 at 720p (exactly 240
rows), photographs the list, a selection and the return, and asserts the stacked mode.
