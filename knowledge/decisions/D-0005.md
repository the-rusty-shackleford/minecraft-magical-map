# D-0005 — Own landmarks are operator teleport targets

Accepted 2026-09-22.

Rusty: "please make landmarks in the magical atlas teleportable to like villages are".
D-0001 had kept personal landmarks as navigation destinations only.

A landmark now resolves through the provider for its owner (it did not resolve at all
before, which is what "Destination is unavailable" would have said) and is stored and
loaded with `teleportable` set, so the atlas shows the operator's Teleport button and the
server's existing gate applies unchanged: permission level 2, re-resolution at request
time, a fully generated destination chunk (D-0004), and a safe arrival. A landmark marked
from the map with unknown height arrives on the local surface; one marked in place keeps
its depth. Another player's landmark still does not resolve for you.

GameTest: an operator arrives at their own stored landmark; a stranger cannot resolve it.
