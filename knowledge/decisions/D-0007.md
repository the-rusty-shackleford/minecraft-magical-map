# D-0007 — The atlas implements the AzimuthLocation protocol

Accepted 2026-09-24. Rusty: atlas icons on the bar, fading in within about 256 blocks; then
"create our own version of the locator bar (call it Azimuth) … make some kind of AzimuthLocation
protocol and then have the atlas implement it."

Azimuth (repo `minecraft-azimuth`) owns the bar and the protocol (its D-0002). Magical Map
implements it in `integration/azimuth/AtlasBearings`, registered by `AzimuthBridge` only when
Azimuth is loaded (the entry checks `ModList` and never names an Azimuth class itself), compiled
against `com.chunkworks.azimuth:azimuth` from mavenLocal, an optional mod dependency.

**Only while the viewer carries an atlas.** Rusty's call. The bar shows what your atlas knows;
without one you see players and compass points only. Azimuth tells a provider only who is
asking and where they stand, so the atlas checks the inventory itself; that is the protocol's
intent (Azimuth's D-0002), not a gap in it.

**What goes on the bar**: every place the atlas would list for that viewer except the players
(Azimuth shows those itself with its own rules): bought villages (public), the viewer's C.A.M.P.
and their own landmarks (each provider's `snapshot(Viewer)` already filters by viewer). Culled to
the viewer's dimension and Azimuth's range here too, so a provider's far places never travel.

**Identity**: the bearing's provider is `magicalmap:atlas` and its id is the atlas provider's own
id and local id joined (`villagedeed:villages/structure:8`), so a place keeps one identity across
the atlas and the bar and the bar's roster deduplicates correctly.

**Isolation**: Azimuth isolates failures per Azimuth provider, so the atlas as one provider would
lose every place on the bar when a single atlas provider threw while the atlas itself kept the
others. Both now take each provider's snapshot through `AtlasServer.places`, which checks the
contract (256 places, own identity, no key twice), drops a failing provider on its own and logs
it once per server; a camp bridge that throws costs the camp, on the atlas and on the bar alike.

Rejected: registering each atlas provider with Azimuth separately (the atlas rule "only while
carried" would have to be repeated in each, and third-party atlas providers would need to know
Azimuth); a Magical Map client config to disable the bar's atlas icons (Azimuth's `bar.enabled`
and the atlas in the inventory are the switches; one more is clutter).

Verified by the integration GameTest (a test destination within range appears on the bar with
an atlas in the inventory, not without one, not beyond range, and the players provider is never
relayed) and by the booth's bar photo over the atlas world with Azimuth loaded.
