# D-0008 — Villages name their owner; the held atlas is a bare minimap in the corner

Accepted 2026-09-24, late. Rusty: "I would like the villages in the atlas to say who they are
owned by as well. And the minimap when the atlas is in your hand but not activated is a little
too zoomed in, kinda useless. Can you make it a bit smaller in size and zoom it out some?" Then:
"For the minimap, get rid of the coordinates (azimuth already gives us that) and the direction,
and the Magical Atlas part. Keep it just the minimap and tuck it into the corner."

**Owner on the village.** The Village Deed bridge (`integration/OptionalLocations`) already read
a claim's deed for the owner's id; it now also binds the claim's `ownerName()` (Village Deed
2.0.x keeps the last name seen) and the village's hint reads "Owned by Jdrum12" instead of
"Owned village", in the atlas's list and detail. The Azimuth bar shows the same place by its
bell; names are the atlas's business.

**The travel view.** The held atlas's HUD was a 142 by 150 framed panel titled "MAGICAL
ATLAS" with a 128 by 100 map at 2 blocks a pixel and a heading-and-coordinates line under it.
It is now the map alone in a frame, 110 by 86, tucked four pixels into the top-right corner:
96 by 72 map pixels at 4 blocks a pixel, 384 by 288 blocks, so a village or a river across the
way is on it. The title, heading and coordinates are gone: Azimuth's bar carries the heading
and the coordinates for everyone. The tracked destination's strip (its name and distance)
stays under the map, since that is the navigation readout, not the map's chrome; Rusty can
have it gone too by saying so.

At the game's minimum width (320 columns) the corner frame overlaps Azimuth's bar by five
pixels; at any ordinary width they are clear of each other.

Rejected: keeping the coordinates in the map's corner (duplicates the bar); a round minimap
(the atlas is sheets of square map pixels; a circle would clip them for no gain); making the
zoom a config (the atlas screen has its own zoom for looking; the held view is a glance).

Verified by the booth's travel photo (`02-travel`) judged by eye, the village fixture's detail
reading "Owned by <name>", and the existing GameTests.
