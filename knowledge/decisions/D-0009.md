# D-0009 — The corner minimap covers 768 by 576 blocks

Accepted 2026-09-26. Rusty, on 0.3.1's bare corner map (D-0008): "the minimap simplification is
great but its still too zoomed in. It needs to show more in the same amount of space."

**The change.** The held atlas's travel view keeps its frame (110 by 86, four pixels into the
top-right corner) and its 96 by 72 map pixels, and draws them at 8 blocks a pixel instead of 4:
768 by 576 blocks, twice the span each way, four times the area. The map's grid follows (a line
every 128 blocks, 16 pixels). Nothing else moves: the tracked destination's strip stays under
the map, the atlas screen's own zoom is untouched.

**Why 8.** The first release's 2 blocks a pixel and 0.3.1's 4 were each "too zoomed in"; the
next doubling is the smallest step that is not another near miss, and it is a vanilla map
scale: a level-3 sheet draws pixel for pixel, a level-4 sheet (every sheet in Rusty's atlas
but one, read off the box's `map_*.dat` scale bytes) draws at two screen pixels per map pixel
instead of four, so the view gets sharper as it gets wider. Icons stay 14 pixels, which is now
112 blocks of ground each, and the 18-pixel grouping already folds icons that would overlap.

Rejected: a larger frame (Rusty asked for more in the same space); a zoom key or config for the
held view (D-0008's reasoning stands: the held view is a glance, the atlas screen is for
looking).

**Verification.** The constant is one line (`AtlasCanvas.HUD_SCALE`). Full clean build green
(18 JUnit, 20 GameTests, booth COMPLETE); the booth's travel photo judged by eye at 3x beside
0.3.1's (`devtools/verification/0.3.2/`): same frame, the sheet at half its width, the grid at
128 blocks, nearby places folding under one icon with a count. The fixture's one scale-0 sheet
cannot show a filled frame; Rusty's scale-4 sheets will.
