# D-0010 — An atlas in a carried bag is carried

Accepted 2026-09-28. Part of Rusty's one change for every mod that looks at a player's inventory
("all things that see your inventory also see your backpack"): the Carried protocol
(`minecraft-carried`, its D-0001), nested in this jar.

D-0007 puts the atlas's places on Azimuth's bar only while the viewer carries an atlas. "Carries"
was the inventory's 41 slots; it is now `Carried.has(player, atlas)`: the inventory's main slots,
the offhand and every carried bag's storage cells. What the atlas does in hand (its pages, the
corner minimap) is unchanged: that is holding, not carrying.

An atlas in a bag does not tick, so it records nothing new while it is there, as an atlas in a
chest would; its places are already the player's.

Verified by the Azimuth bridge's GameTest, extended on its own player: with the atlas moved into a
Backpacks+ bag in a main slot (Backpacks+ 0.6.0 on the gametest server) the destination is still on
the bar. Failed on 0.3.2.
